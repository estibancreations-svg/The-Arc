# THE ARC

**Format:** Premium one-hour science-fiction drama
**Primary setting:** ARC-01 — Passover-Class Master Arc
**Status:** Confirmed as its own standalone project (2026-09-17) — peer in scale/priority to the CEO Master Dashboard project, separate from "StarTrek" (a different, still-undefined systems/AI project) and separate from any other franchise.

## Logline

Inside a civilization-scale artificial world, an unscheduled systems activation exposes a hidden survival protocol and forces a systems ecologist, a ring mechanic, and a civic investigator to decide whether the Arc is malfunctioning, protecting them, or preparing for something outside.

## The Hook

The Arc is a ship, a city, a country, an ecosystem, and a machine. Its five branches carry homes, food systems, water systems, transit, civic power, hidden service decks, and under-panel oceans. When the ship begins transforming itself without authorization, every technical question becomes a political and moral one.

## Franchise Shape

The Arc is not one spaceship — it is a family of civilization-scale artificial worlds (five classes so far), each solving the same problem differently: how to move civilization, water, atmosphere, food, culture, industry, and life through space without reducing the setting to hallways and control rooms. It expands into ship classes, books, graphic novels, art books, games, VR, collectibles, and a Digital Twin/VisionWeaver production system that keeps the world consistent across stories and media.
